FAQ

The questions parents actually ask.

Straight answers about pricing, format, and how the programs work. Anything we missed, bring to the consultation — or just send us a message.

Pricing & packages

How much does it cost?

We sell programs, not hours, so the price depends on the outcome you're buying. The Minimum Package (a personalized self-paced study plan plus monthly practice tests) runs a low monthly rate; the Score Builder and Premium Package programs are one-time program fees that cover everything — sessions, practice tests, materials, and reporting. Current ranges are listed on the Programs page, and your exact quote comes from the consultation.

Why don't you just charge by the hour?

Because hourly billing rewards the wrong thing. When a tutor is paid per hour, the incentive is more hours — not a finished result. Our programs have a defined goal and a defined end date, which puts the accountability where it belongs: on us. You know the total cost up front, and it never quietly grows.

What exactly is the Minimum Package?

It's our deliberately low-cost entry point, and it's exactly two things: a personalized, self-paced study plan built for your student, and a monthly timed ACT practice test with a full score report so you can see whether the plan is working. It does not include tutoring sessions of any kind — that's what keeps it affordable. It's a genuinely good fit for self-motivated students, and many families start there before upgrading to one-on-one.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. The Score Builder and Premium Package program fees can be split into monthly installments over the length of the program. We'll go over the options on your consultation call.

Can we upgrade or switch packages mid-program?

Yes. Families often start with the Minimum Package and move up to one-on-one once they see the structure working. When you upgrade, what you've already paid counts toward the larger program — you're never penalized for starting small.

Format & logistics

Are sessions online or in person?

Most sessions run online over video with a shared digital whiteboard, which lets us match your student to the best tutor for their need rather than the closest one. In-person options may be available depending on your location — ask on the consultation call.

Who will actually be tutoring my student?

Two people, in two different roles. Jon Jay Moore, our founder, designs your student's program personally and reviews every progress report before it reaches you. The sessions themselves are run by a tutor Jon Jay selects — deliberately a recent high school graduate, because the material is still fresh in their head and students find someone a few years ahead of them far more relatable than an older instructor. If a match isn't clicking, we change it. No drama, no restart fee.

How long until we see results?

Practice-test scores typically start moving within the first four to six weeks, once the diagnostic-driven plan takes hold. That's also why our shortest one-on-one program runs about three months — real, durable score gains need at least one full cycle of learn, practice, test, and adjust.

How involved do I need to be as a parent?

Less than you'd think. You'll get progress reports on a regular cadence and a mid-program check-in call. The structure, scheduling, and accountability are our job — most parents tell us the best part is not having to be the homework police anymore.

What if my student misses a session?

Life happens — sessions rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice are made up at no charge. Since you've bought a program rather than hours, a missed week doesn't burn money; we adjust the plan to keep the goal on track.

Fit & expectations

My student is unmotivated. Will this still work?

Often, yes — because "unmotivated" is usually what stuck looks like from the outside. Visible progress is the best motivator we know: when a student watches their practice score climb, effort follows. That said, we're honest in the consultation. If we don't think the timing is right, we'll say so.

Do you guarantee a specific score increase?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific number on an official test, and we'd be skeptical of anyone who does. What we do promise is movement, and we put money behind it: if your student's first three practice tests in their first 12 weeks show no improvement, your next month is free. Beyond that you get a real diagnostic, a plan built for your student's target, measurement you can see, and a program we only take on if we believe the goal is reachable.

Do you tutor the whole ACT?

No — and that's deliberate. We tutor ACT Math and the high school math behind it, period. On the enhanced ACT, the composite is the average of the English, Math, and Reading scores (Science is optional and doesn't count toward the composite), so math alone is a full third of the score — roughly every 3 points of math improvement moves the composite by about 1 point. It's also the most trainable section, and a practice focused on one section beats a generalist spread across all of them. If your student needs more composite points than math alone can deliver, we'll tell you that plainly on the consultation call.

We only need help with math class, not the ACT. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Our math programs stand on their own for Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Precalculus. The bonus is that this is exactly the math the ACT tests — so if your student takes the ACT later, the foundation is already built.

What happens on the consultation call?

It's a focused call with Jon Jay, our founder — set aside up to 45 minutes so nothing gets rushed. We'll talk through your student's current scores and grades, their target schools or goals, and their timeline. The call ends with a specific program recommendation and a start date — including, sometimes, the recommendation not to buy anything yet. Lesson one then opens with a full timed diagnostic (one ACT math section on the enhanced format, under an hour), and if it shows something different, we adjust the plan. There's no charge and no obligation. Book yours here.
