How we measure

No vibes. Just numbers.

Every result we report follows the same method, so improvements mean what they say.

  • 01
    Real baseline first

    Every program starts with a full-length, timed diagnostic — official practice material, real conditions. That's the "before."

  • 02
    Tracked continuously

    Practice tests on a fixed cadence, with section-level breakdowns, so trends show up early — good or bad.

  • 03
    Reported honestly

    The "after" is a timed practice test taken under the same conditions as the diagnostic — the same measuring stick at both ends, not a best-day score. Parents see the same reports we do.

The 12-week promise

We keep score with practice tests, so we'll stand behind them: if your student's first 3 practice tests in their first 12 weeks show no improvement, your next month is on us.
