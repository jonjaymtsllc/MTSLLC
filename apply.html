Who we're looking for

The bar is high. That's the point.

Parents trust us with their student's score because our tutors have recently done exactly what their student is trying to do.

  • 01
    You graduated high school recently

    You took the ACT within the last few years, so the test — and what it feels like to sit it — is still fresh.

  • 02
    You scored 33+ on ACT Math

    A 33 or higher on the math section is our minimum. You'll share your score report as part of your application.

    You show up on time, prepared, and you can explain — not just solve. Students should leave sessions more confident, not more confused.
