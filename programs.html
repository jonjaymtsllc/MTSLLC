What we teach

Two tracks, one integrated system

ACT Math Prep

A focused campaign on the section worth a full third of the score: on the enhanced ACT, the composite is the average of English, Math, and Reading — so roughly every 3 points of math improvement moves the composite by about 1 point. We build content mastery across the tested Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Precalculus material, plus timing and question triage, timed section drills, and score tracking toward a target math subscore.

Best started 3–6 months before your student's target test date.

High School Math

Course-aligned tutoring in Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Precalculus — the exact math the ACT tests. We work from your student's actual class material, so the semester grade improves and the ACT math foundation gets built at the same time.

Available on its own or blended into any ACT package.
