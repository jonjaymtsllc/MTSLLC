About us

Built because hourly tutoring wasn't working.

Moore Tutoring Solutions is a team of ACT and math specialists with one shared conviction: families shouldn't pay for time — they should pay for progress.

Our story

The pattern we kept seeing

Moore Tutoring Solutions started the way most tutoring does: one tutor, one whiteboard, hourly sessions. And we kept watching the same story repeat — a family hires a tutor, the student "does an hour" every week, everyone feels productive, and six months later the score hasn't moved.

The problem wasn't the students, and it usually wasn't the tutors. It was the model. Hourly tutoring has no finish line, no defined goal, and no one accountable for the outcome. The incentive is to keep the sessions going, not to end them with a result.

So we rebuilt the whole thing. We designed programs instead of sessions — each with a target at the end and measurement in between. And we split the work into two roles: Jon Jay Moore, our founder, designs and reviews every program personally, while sessions are delivered by contracted tutors — recent high school graduates chosen because the material is still fresh in their heads and students genuinely relate to them. And we put a number on everything, because what gets measured gets improved.

Our mission

Make the outcome the product

Every decision we make runs through one filter: does this move the student's number — their ACT composite, their math grade, their confidence walking into the test?

  • 01
    Specialists over generalists

    We do one thing: ACT Math and the coursework behind it. A practice focused on the section worth a third of the composite beats a generalist spread across four.

  • 02
    Honesty over upsells

    We recommend the smallest program that gets the job done — and we turn away engagements we don't believe in.

  • 03
    Parents in the loop

    You get the same progress data we use internally. No "trust us" — just reports.
